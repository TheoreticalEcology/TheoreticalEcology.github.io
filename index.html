Theoretical Ecology

University of Regensburg · Prof. Dr. Florian Hartig

We use theory, computer simulations and statistics / data science to understand patterns and mechanisms in ecology, evolution and conservation — in particular species interactions, community assembly and biodiversity patterns across scales.
